How it works

Per project, with a defined deliverable, rather than an open-ended block of hours. For anything unfamiliar or already broken, I would rather start with a short paid diagnosis than quote a fix blind. It is cheaper for you and more honest than a guess.

I work remotely, on my own equipment, and I am straightforward about what I do not know yet. Rates on request, and they depend on the shape of the work.

Get in touch

If you have a system that nearly works, or an idea that needs someone to actually build it, tell me what it is and what it is supposed to do. I will tell you honestly whether I am the right person for it.
